# HoldCo OS | 1-Page Summary
**Executive Overview**

### **Purpose**
HoldCo OS is a centralized management system for business groups. it simplifies the legal, financial, and compliance burden of overseeing multiple companies from a single secure dashboard.

### **Core Capabilities**
*   **Entity Registry:** Universal folder for all company details & CIPC data.
*   **Governance Tracking:** Register of Directors, Shareholders, and Beneficial Owners.
*   **Compliance Hub:** Central tracking for SARS, CIPC, and Labor law deadlines with a calendar view.
*   **Smart Document Vault:** Secure, categorized storage for MOIs, Certificates, and Tax papers.
*   **Relationship Mapping:** visual representation of group ownership hierarchies.
*   **Financial Pulse:** Cross-group cash monitoring and opening balance logs.

### **Tech Architecture**
*   **Frontend:** React 19 + Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion (Responsive & Mobile-Ready).
*   **Backend:** Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, RLS, Storage).
*   **Security:** Multi-layer security with User-Level Data Isolation.

### **Value for Stakeholders**
*   **Founders:** Full transparency on group valuation and legal standing.
*   **Admin/Ops:** 80% reduction in time spent hunting for documents or registry details.
*   **Investors:** Instantly accessible "Due Diligence" room.
